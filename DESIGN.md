# Design System: Spur

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Quiet Line"**

Spur is a working tool for a small circle of people who make and share short links, and its surfaces stay quiet so the links and their numbers lead. A cool stone neutral family runs through two composed themes: a pale cool ground under white panels in light, a near-black ground under one-step-lighter panels in dark. Panels are flat and ruled off by hairlines, the one primary action is near-black on light and near-white on dark, and the type never raises its voice. Against that quiet, one device speaks: the line map, where each collection is a thin line in its own colour and its links sit on it as stations sized by clicks. The line colours are the only saturated colour in the interface, and they always mean a collection or a tag.

The interface is an operating tool, not a showcase. Density is moderate and even: 36px controls, 12px table cell padding, 20px gaps between panels, a 1360px page. Standard web controls are used throughout; the system contributes the two themes, the two typefaces, the density, one signature component and one copper accent that marks exactly three things. Motion is short and confined to state changes, and page content does not animate in on load.

Spur began as a Tashkent Metro station, with a granite bar, marble floor, bronze lettering and a band of ceramic tile, and was quietened at the owner's request: the materials became role tokens, the ornament went, and the metro survives only as the line map and the branch in the wordmark. It refuses the category default of a grey sidebar, a blue accent, rows of stat tiles and a lone line chart, and it refuses ornament.

**Key Characteristics:**
- A quiet top bar on the surface colour, ruled off by a hairline, over a pale cool ground in light and a near-black ground in dark.
- Two composed themes chosen as System, Light or Dark; dark is its own set of steps, not an inversion.
- Flat panels with 1px hairlines and no resting shadow, the create panel included.
- Seven line colours reserved for collection and tag identity, assigned in a fixed order and tuned per theme.
- One copper accent for today's bar, the spike rule and the wordmark's dot, and nothing else.
- Golos Text for the interface; Red Hat Mono for short codes, the short-link host and click times.
- The line map: collections drawn as 2px coloured lines with links as stations sized by clicks.
- 150 to 200ms ease-out on state changes; one 240ms arrival when a new link is created.

### Named Rules
**The Line Colour Rule.** Line colours mean a collection or a tag and nothing else. Buttons, links, focus rings, selection, charts, status and decoration never use them.

**The Fixed Order Rule.** Line colours are offered and assigned in the order red, blue, green, violet, amber, teal, with slate for no collection. The order is part of the identity; do not reshuffle it per screen or per theme.

**The Copper Marks Rule.** Copper marks three things: today's bar, the spike onset rule and the wordmark's dot. The spike label is the only copper text, and it uses Copper Ink. Nothing else in the interface is copper.

**The Composed Dark Rule.** Dark is its own set of steps tuned on its own ground, not an inversion of light. Components name a role token, never a hex, so both themes follow; the only literals are the white label on a danger button and the white check on a colour swatch, which stay white in both themes.

**The Quiet Health Rule.** A healthy link shows no badge. Status colour appears only when a link is paused, expired, expiring within a week or in error.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Golos Text Variable (with Golos Text, system-ui, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Golos Text Variable (same stack)
**Label/Mono Font:** Red Hat Mono Variable (with Red Hat Mono, ui-monospace, monospace)

**Character:** Golos Text is a plain grotesque with full Latin and Cyrillic, set with stylistic set 1 (`ss01`) on all text and held to quiet weights: 400 for text, 500 to 600 for labels and headings, 650 only in the wordmark. Red Hat Mono marks short codes as names and makes the host prefix exactly measurable in `ch`.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600, 2.75rem, line-height 1, -0.02em): today's click count, the single large figure in a clicks panel. One per panel, never a row of them.
- **Headline** (600, 1.5rem, 1.2): page titles (Dashboard, Links, Collections, a link's name) and the sign-in card title.
- **Subhead** (600, 1.25rem, 1.2): section headings above a group of panels, such as "Clicks" on a link page.
- **Title** (600, 1rem, 1.2): panel and dialog titles.
- **Body** (400, 0.9375rem, 1.5): default text. Explanatory text is capped at 52 to 68ch.
- **Body Small** (400, 0.875rem, 1.5): table cells, inputs, sentences inside panels, menu items, toasts.
- **Control** (560, 0.875rem, line-height 1): button labels. Small buttons drop to 0.8125rem; large ones rise to 0.9375rem.
- **Nav** (500, 0.875rem): top bar nav items.
- **Label** (560, 0.8125rem): field labels, segments, tag toggles, the "More options" toggle. Hints and meta lines use the same size at 400.
- **Column Label** (500, 0.8125rem, Ink 3, sentence case): table column headers.
- **Caption** (400, 0.75rem): the map key, secondary lines in tooltips, the "View as table" toggle. Badges use this size at 600, tag chips at 560.
- **Code** (Red Hat Mono 500, 0.875rem, tabular figures): short codes, the `url.mekhroj.com/` host prefix, click timestamps. Station codes on the map and times in Recent clicks drop to 0.8125rem.
- **Code Specimen** (Red Hat Mono 600, 1.25rem, 1.2): a link's short code set large on its page, host prefix at 1rem in Ink 3.
- **Wordmark** (650, 1.125rem, -0.01em): "Spur" beside its mark, in the top bar and above the sign-in card.

Headings use `text-wrap: balance`. Figures in tables and charts use tabular numerals and sit right-aligned; click counts in tables are 600. Chart tick labels are 11px.

### Named Rules
**The Code Face Rule.** Anything a person would type after the slash, and the host before it, is set in Red Hat Mono. Everything else is Golos Text.

**The Quiet Label Rule.** Labels are sentence case and muted: column headers are Ink 3 at 500, so the figures under them lead. Nothing in the interface is uppercase or letter-spaced; the only tracking is negative, on the wordmark and the display figure.

## Layout

Pages sit in a centred container of 1360px with 24px side padding, 32px above and 48px below. At 820px and below the padding becomes 20px above, 16px at the sides and 40px below. Spacing is on a 4px module (4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48).

Vertical rhythm: 24px between the major blocks of the dashboard (16px at 760px and below), 20px between blocks on other pages, 20px gaps between panels in a grid. Inside a panel: 16px above the header, 20px at the sides and bottom, 16px between header and body. Field labels sit 6px above their control; form rows are 12 to 16px apart.

The dashboard opens with the full-width create panel, then the heading with its 7 / 30 / 90 day control and a collection filter, then a two-column row (the Collections map at 1.75fr beside the Clicks panel at a minimum of 320px), then a three-column row (Top links, sources, recent clicks). At 1180px the top row stacks with the Clicks panel first and the bottom row becomes two columns with Top links across both; at 760px everything is one column. The link page runs a two-column panel grid that stacks at 860px. Collections, tags, invites and settings are a single column capped at 920px.

The top bar is sticky, 56px tall, and shares the 1360px container with 24px gaps between its groups. The search field hides at 1080px. At 820px the nav moves behind a menu button, the Shorten button keeps only its plus icon, and the nav opens as a list under the bar. The sign-in and invite screens have no bar: the wordmark sits above a 400px card that starts between 40px and 128px from the top (14vh).

Tables scroll horizontally inside their panel and shed lower-priority columns as the width drops (at 1100px and 720px) rather than wrapping. The first and last columns sit flush with the panel's padding.

Control heights: 36px by default, 30px small, 44px for the create field and its button, 32px for icon buttons, the search field and the account button, 28px for segments, small icon buttons and tag toggles, 22px for badges and chips.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. Depth comes from tone: in light, white surfaces sit on a pale cool ground; in dark, surfaces sit one step above a near-black ground and raised layers (dialogs, the account menu) one step above that. Every panel is separated from the ground by a 1px hairline and has no shadow at rest, the create panel and the sign-in card included. Shadows exist only for things that float above the page and for the selected segment.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Overlay** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgb(27 30 35 / 0.14), 0 2px 6px rgb(27 30 35 / 0.06)` · dark `0 8px 24px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.5), 0 2px 6px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.3)`): dialogs, the account menu, the chart tooltip, toasts, the sticky bulk-action bar.
- **Selected segment** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgb(27 30 35 / 0.12)` · dark `0 1px 2px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.4)`): the selected segment inside its trough.
- **Current page rule** (`box-shadow: inset 0 -2px 0` in Ink): a 2px ink underline at the bottom edge of the bar under the current nav item.
- **Station halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px` in Surface): cuts the line behind a station disc; an expiring station adds a 2px Warn ring outside it.

Dialog backdrops use the Backdrop colour.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Panel Rule.** Every panel rests flat on the ground with a hairline, the create panel and the sign-in card included. Only floating layers cast the overlay shadow.

## Shapes

Corners are gently rounded and never pill-shaped on containers: 4px for segments, skeletons and small marks; 6px for buttons, inputs, menu items, tooltips and toasts; 10px for panels, dialogs, menus and the bulk bar. Badges, tag chips, tag toggles, the line map terminus and scrollbar thumbs are full pills (999px); the account button is a 32px circle.

Lines are 2px with rounded ends wherever they appear: the line map track and its 2 by 12px end bar, the line mark beside a collection's name, the line sample on the Collections page, and the strokes of the wordmark.

Circles carry identity. A tag is a filled line dot (10px; 7px inside a tag chip, 8px in a tag toggle, 14px in the tags list). A collection is a line mark: a short 2px length of its line ending in a 10px ring, 16 by 10px overall, and on the Collections page a longer sample ending in a 14px station. Stations on the map are Surface discs with a 2px ring in the line colour and a 2px Surface halo. Colour swatches are 28px discs with a 2px Surface gap and a 1px Hairline Strong ring; the chosen one gets a 2px Ink ring and a check.

Borders are 1px throughout. Within a panel, rows are divided by 1px Sunken; the panel edge and table header rule are 1px Hairline.

## Components

### Buttons
Solid and plain. The primary fill is for the main action on a surface; danger is filled only for deletes.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (6px), 36px tall, 16px side padding, 560 weight label, 8px icon gap.
- **Primary:** Primary Fill with On Primary text; hover to Primary Hover.
- **Secondary:** Surface with a 1px Hairline Strong border and Ink text; hover to a Control Edge border on Subtle. The default variant, and the top bar's Shorten button at small size with a plus icon.
- **Ghost:** transparent with Ink 2 text; hover to Sunken with Ink text. Used for icon buttons (32px square, 28px small) and the mobile menu button.
- **Danger:** Danger Fill with a white label; hover to Danger Fill Hover. In confirmation dialogs and the bulk-action bar.
- **Sizes:** small 30px (12px padding, 0.8125rem), large 44px (20px padding, 0.9375rem); the create panel's Shorten is large with a minimum width of 112px.
- **Disabled:** 55% opacity, not-allowed cursor. **Loading:** a 14px ring spinner in the current text colour turns before the label.
- **Transitions:** background, border and text colour at 150ms on the shared ease-out.

### Chips and Badges
- **Tag chip:** Surface pill, 22px, 1px Hairline border, Ink 2 text at 0.75rem 560, led by a 7px line dot in the tag's colour.
- **Tag toggle:** Surface pill, 28px, 1px Hairline Strong border, 8px line dot; hover to a Control Edge border. Pressed fills with Primary Fill and On Primary text, and the dot gives way to a check.
- **Status badge:** 22px pill in a status pair, 0.75rem 600, always a 12px icon followed by a word.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 10px.
- **Background:** Surface on the Ground.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none at rest; see Elevation & Depth.
- **Border:** 1px Hairline.
- **Internal Padding:** 16px top, 20px sides and bottom; the title row is a flex row with actions (a text link or a segmented control) at the right.
- **Create panel:** the same flat panel at 20px 24px (16px at 760px and below), full width at the top of the dashboard. Where it is on the page, the top bar's Shorten button and the `n` key focus its URL field instead of opening the create dialog.
- **Stale data:** while a panel refetches, its previous content dims to 60% opacity over 200ms instead of being replaced by a skeleton. Skeletons (a Sunken to Subtle shimmer, 1.4s) appear only on first load.
- **Empty state:** left-aligned title at 0.9375rem and a sentence of up to 52ch that explains what will appear and how to get it.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** Field background, 36px, 1px Control Edge border, 6px radius, 12px side padding, Ink 3 placeholder. Hover darkens the border to Ink 3.
- **Focus:** 2px Focus outline inset by 1px, border to Focus.
- **Error:** border to Danger ink; the message sits below the control in Danger ink with an icon. Form-level errors are a Danger tint block with 12px padding.
- **Disabled:** Subtle background.
- **Short code field:** the `url.mekhroj.com/` host is an inset prefix in Red Hat Mono, Ink 3; the input text is Red Hat Mono, and its left padding is calculated in `ch` so the code starts exactly after the slash. In the create panel both fields are 44px at 0.9375rem.
- **Select:** the same box with a 12px chevron drawn in Ink 2 for each theme and 32px right padding.
- **Segmented control:** a Sunken trough with 3px padding and 2px gaps; segments are 28px, 4px radius, Ink 2 text; the selected segment is Segment On with Ink text and the selected-segment shadow. Used for date ranges, for switching a panel's breakdown, and for the theme switch.

### Navigation
- **Top bar:** sticky, 56px, Surface with a 1px Hairline rule below. From left: the Spur wordmark, the five nav items, the search field, the Shorten button, and a 32px round account button with initials.
- **Nav items:** the full height of the bar, 12px padding, Ink 2 text at 0.875rem 500. Hover turns the text Ink; the current page is Ink with a 2px Ink underline at the bar's bottom edge. No background fill.
- **Search:** 240px by 32px, Subtle with a Hairline border, Ink 3 icon and placeholder; hover to Hairline Strong; focus switches to Field with the focus ring. The `/` key focuses it.
- **Account button:** a Sunken circle with a Hairline border and initials at 0.75rem 600; hover to a Control Edge border.
- **Account menu:** a Raised popover, 1px Hairline, 10px radius, overlay shadow, at least 240px wide, anchored to the right edge of the page container. It holds the name and email, a Theme row with the System / Light / Dark segmented control, Settings, and Sign out.
- **Mobile:** below 820px the nav moves behind a ghost menu button and opens under the bar as a list of 40px items (the nav plus Settings), set off by a Sunken rule; the current item takes a Subtle background in place of the underline.

### Theme Switch
- **Choice:** System, Light or Dark, from the account menu or the Appearance panel in Settings (the same segmented control, with a hint that System follows the device and that the choice is saved in this browser).
- **Behaviour:** the theme is applied to the root element before the first paint, so neither theme flashes on load. System follows the device's setting live, a change in one tab reaches the others, and the browser's theme colour follows Surface.

### Wordmark
The only logo. A 20px mark, a 2px line with a short branch curving up from it to a copper dot, followed by "Spur" in the Wordmark style in Ink. The strokes follow the text colour, so they change with the theme; only the dot is Copper. The favicon is the same mark on a 32px rounded square (7px) of light Primary Fill, with Float Ink strokes and the dark Copper dot, and it does not change with the theme.

### Line Map
The signature component. Each collection is a row: a label column (150 to 200px) with the line mark, the collection name at 560 linking to its filtered links list, and a meta line of link and click counts indented to the name in Ink 3; then the track. Rows are ordered by clicks and divided by a 1px Sunken rule. A key above the map, in Caption type and Ink 3, explains size and both states.
- **Track:** a 2px line in the collection's colour with a 2 by 12px end bar at the right, drawn behind the stations at the centre of the largest marker.
- **Stations:** links on the line, ordered by clicks, each with an equal share of the track up to 112px; the track holds one station per 112px (84px on tracks under 480px). Each is a Surface disc with a 2px ring in the line colour; disc size runs from 12px to 26px on a square-root scale of clicks in the selected range, on one scale for the whole map. A station with no clicks gets a 1.5px ring.
- **Station names:** the short code below each disc, Red Hat Mono 0.8125rem in Ink 2, truncated with an ellipsis.
- **States:** a closed station (paused or expired) has a dashed Ink 3 ring and an Ink 3 code; a station expiring within a week gets an extra 2px Warn ring outside its halo.
- **Overflow:** when a line has more links than fit, the last slot is a terminus pill (24px tall, 1.5px line-colour border) reading "+n" and linking to the filtered list.
- **Highlight:** hovering or focusing a station or its Top links row, or hovering one of its Recent clicks entries, highlights the link everywhere on the page: the station's disc fills with the line colour and scales to 1.12 over 200ms, its code turns Ink at 600, and its rows in Top links and Recent clicks take a Subtle background. A focused or hovered station also shows a Float tooltip with the link name, its clicks and any state in Float Warn. Clicking opens the link's page.
- **Arrival:** a newly created link's station scales in from 0.3 with a fade over 240ms. This is the only authored entrance in the app; the confirmation strip in the create form uses the same 240ms timing.
- **Narrow screens:** below 640px the label stacks above the track.

### Clicks Chart
Today's count leads in Display type with "clicks today" beside it and a sentence for the range below. The daily strip is Chart Bar at 82% opacity with today's bar in Copper at full strength; a hovered bar turns Chart Bar Active. A 1px Chart Quiet baseline, a Sunken gridline and Ink 3 tick labels frame it. The onset of the latest spike is a 1.5px Copper rule, continued above the plot by the "Spike from" label in Copper Ink at 0.75rem 600. Hover shows a Float tooltip with the overlay shadow and an Ink 3 crosshair. A "View as table" text toggle in Ink 2, underlined in Hairline Strong, reveals the same data as a table.
- **Breakdowns:** rows of name (with an Ink 3 icon), an 8px Chart Bar on a Subtle track, and the count with its share in Ink 3.
- **Hours:** 24 bars, 72px tall, with 3px top corners in Chart Bar; the peak hour is Chart Bar Active.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** reference role tokens (Surface, Ink 2, Hairline) in every component so light and dark both follow, and give any new token its own dark step.
- **Do** use Primary Fill for the one main action on a surface, and Ink for the focus ring, the current-page underline and active chart marks.
- **Do** show a collection as a line mark and a tag as a line dot (10px, 7px in chips), always next to its name in text.
- **Do** draw line colours on Surface; light Line Amber clears 3:1 there only just (3.12:1).
- **Do** set short codes, the host prefix and click times in Red Hat Mono, with the host in Ink 3 and the code in Ink.
- **Do** separate panels from the ground with a 1px Hairline, and rows inside panels with 1px Sunken.
- **Do** keep state transitions at 150 to 200ms on `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, and respect reduced motion.
- **Do** pair every status colour with an icon and a word.
- **Do** dim stale data to 60% while it refetches rather than flashing a skeleton.
- **Do** keep creating a link one step away: the Shorten button in the top bar on every signed-in page, and the `n` key anywhere.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use a line colour for a button, link, focus ring, chart bar, status or decoration.
- **Don't** add ornament: no tile bands, patterns, textures or decorative colour strips; the line map is the one signature device.
- **Don't** use Copper for anything but today's bar, the spike rule and label, and the wordmark's dot.
- **Don't** derive the dark theme by inverting or filtering light values; each role has its own dark step.
- **Don't** put a shadow on a resting panel, the create panel included; Overlay is for floating layers only.
- **Don't** set labels in uppercase or letter-spaced type.
- **Don't** add entrance animation to page content on load; panels and rows appear without motion.
- **Don't** show a badge on a healthy link.
